Overview

BioMaxxx ("we," "our," or "the Platform") is a personal AI-assisted health monitoring application that integrates wearable biometric data, AI-powered vision diagnostics, COPD/AQI tracking, and biofeedback stress management tools.

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of BioMaxxx, including the web application, any API endpoints, and any connected third-party health data sources (including WHOOP).

🛡️
Our Core Commitment: BioMaxxx is designed for personal health awareness only. We never sell your health data. We never share it with advertisers. We never use it for any purpose beyond running the features you explicitly enable.

03

WHOOP Integration & Biometric Data

BioMaxxx integrates with the official WHOOP Developer API v2 (api.prod.whoop.com) using the OAuth 2.0 Authorization Code flow. This integration is strictly read-only in every respect.

✅ What We Access from WHOOP (Read-Only)
  • Recovery score, HRV (RMSSD), resting heart rate, SpO₂, skin temperature
  • Day strain, calorie burn, average/max heart rate per physiological cycle
  • Sleep performance, sleep stages (light, deep/SWS, REM, awake), respiratory rate
  • Workout strain, sport type, heart rate zones
  • Basic profile (first name, last name, email — for display only)
🚫 What We NEVER Do with WHOOP
  • We never write, modify, or delete any data on your WHOOP account
  • We never share your WHOOP data with third parties
  • We never use your WHOOP data for advertising or profiling
  • We never store raw WHOOP tokens in browser storage (localStorage/cookies)
  • We never request write scopes from the WHOOP API

OAuth Scopes Requested: offline, read:recovery, read:cycles, read:workout, read:sleep, read:profile, read:body_measurement

You may revoke BioMaxxx's access to your WHOOP data at any time from your WHOOP app settings or by clicking Disconnect in the BioMaxxx IoT Hub panel.

Important: BioMaxxx is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by WHOOP, Inc. WHOOP® is a registered trademark of WHOOP, Inc. Your WHOOP account is governed by WHOOP's own Privacy Policy.
04

Camera & Vision AI Data

BioMaxxx's AI Dry Eye Scanner and AI Nail Deficiency Scanner use your device's camera to perform real-time health assessments. We take an edge-first, privacy-preserving approach:

📷 How Camera Data Works
  • Processing is 100% in-browser — all analysis runs via MediaPipe Face Landmarker on your device
  • Video frames are never uploaded to any server
  • No images or video are stored — frames are processed in memory and immediately discarded
  • Only the numeric results (e.g., blink rate, eye openness score) are sent to the backend for logging — not any imagery
  • Camera access is only activated when you explicitly tap "Start Scan" and is released when the session ends

Your browser will show a camera permission prompt. You may deny camera access — this will disable the visual scanning features but all other app features remain available.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data for the following purposes only:

  • Dashboard Display: Show your current WHOOP metrics, BMI results, AQI readings, and health scores
  • Alert Generation: Trigger COPD/AQI spike warnings based on your configured thresholds
  • Trend Analysis: Display 7-day rolling charts of your biometric history
  • Personalized Recommendations: Calculate dietary macros and exercise suggestions based on your body data
  • Session Logging: Record biofeedback game sessions to track stress reduction over time

We do not use your data for: advertising, profiling, machine learning model training on your identifiable data, sale to third parties, or any purpose not listed above.

06

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in a Supabase PostgreSQL database hosted on AWS infrastructure in the Asia South (Mumbai) region.

🔐 Security Measures
  • All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3
  • Database connections use SSL with certificate verification
  • OAuth tokens are stored server-side only — never in browser localStorage or cookies
  • API endpoints enforce CORS and standard security headers (X-Content-Type-Options, X-Frame-Options, CSP)
  • No plaintext passwords stored — authentication delegated to WHOOP OAuth
  • Database row-level security enforced via Supabase RLS policies
